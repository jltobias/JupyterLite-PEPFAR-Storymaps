# Map Tour sources and location choices

This tour is an original, selected narrative. It is not a complete history of HIV or PEPFAR. The first two stops supply context before PEPFAR began in 2003. The other stops describe PEPFAR actions or partnerships.

| PEPFAR announcement | January 28, 2003 | Central Washington, D.C. | [HIV.gov timeline](https://www.hiv.gov/hiv-basics/overview/history/hiv-and-aids-timeline/) |

Coordinates in [tour.js](tour.js) are approximate city centers or national capital anchors. They do not represent individual patients, precise facilities, or exact event addresses. Dates retain the precision supported by the source. Map pins do not encode HIV incidence, prevalence, or risk.

The presentation uses a guided map-and-narrative format inspired by [Esri's *Endangered Languages* StoryMap](https://storymaps.esri.com/stories/endangered-languages/). Its content, design, map, and media were not copied. The tour uses [Leaflet](https://leafletjs.com/) and [OpenStreetMap tiles](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). The live map needs an internet connection; the source-linked narrative still works if the map library fails.
